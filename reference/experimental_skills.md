# Edexcel IGCSE Physics (4PH1) - Experimental Skills Assessment Criteria

Students are tested on their ability to:
1. Solve problems set in a practical context.
2. Apply scientific knowledge and understanding in questions with a practical context.
3. Devise and plan investigations, selecting appropriate techniques.
4. Demonstrate or describe appropriate experimental & investigative methods (including safety).
5. Make observations and measurements with appropriate precision, record methodically, and present appropriately.
6. Identify independent, dependent, and control variables.
7. Analyse and interpret data to draw conclusions consistent with evidence.
8. Communicate findings using appropriate technical language, calculations, and graphs.
9. Assess the reliability of an experimental activity.
10. Evaluate data and methods taking into account factors affecting accuracy and validity.
