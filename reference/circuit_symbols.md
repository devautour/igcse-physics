# Edexcel IGCSE Physics (4PH1) - Electrical Circuit Symbols

## Appendix 8: Electrical circuit symbols

The following are the circuit symbols used in examination papers, as extracted from
the specification. Although these are the forms of circuit symbols that will be used
in examination papers, there may be other internationally agreed symbols which are
acceptable in student answers.

| Description | Symbol (description) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Conductors crossing with no connection | Two straight conductors crossing at right angles, no dot at the intersection |
| Junction of conductors | A conductor branching at a right angle, marked with a solid dot at the junction |
| Open switch | Two contact points joined by a lever raised clear of the second contact |
| Cell | One long thin line (positive terminal) and one short thick line (negative terminal), close together |
| Battery of cells | Two cell symbols in series, separated to indicate two or more cells |
| Power supply (DC) | Two terminals, labelled "D.C." |
| Power supply (AC) | Two terminals joined by a sine wave, labelled "A.C." |
| Transformer | Two coils of wire wound around a shared iron core (two parallel lines between the coils) |
| Ammeter | A circle enclosing the letter "A" |
| Voltmeter | A circle enclosing the letter "V" |
| Fixed resistor | A plain rectangle |
| Variable resistor | A rectangle with a diagonal arrow drawn through it |
| Heater | A rectangle containing vertical hatching |
| Thermistor | A rectangle with a plain diagonal line through it |
| Light-dependent resistor (LDR) | A rectangle with two arrows pointing in towards it |
| Diode | A triangle pointing towards a vertical bar |
| Light-emitting diode (LED) | A diode symbol with two arrows pointing away from it |
| Lamp | A circle enclosing a cross |
| Loudspeaker | A small rectangle attached to a trapezoid cone |
| Microphone | A capsule shape (rounded top, flat base) |
| Electric bell | A dome shape on a baseplate |
| Earth or ground | A vertical line ending in a set of horizontal bars of decreasing length |
| Motor | A circle enclosing the letter "M" |
| Generator | A circle enclosing the letter "G" |
| Fuse/circuit breaker | A rectangle with the connecting wire drawn straight through it |

Source: Pearson Edexcel International GCSE in Physics – Specification – Issue 2 –
April 2018, Appendix 8 (p. 53).
